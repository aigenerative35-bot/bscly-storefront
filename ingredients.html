Questions we are asked

The honest answers.

All topical products are formulated with lick-safety in mind. The paw balms use food-grade ingredients. Rinse-off products are safe at residue levels after drying. No toxic essential oils, no zinc oxide, no camphor.

A dog's skin sits around 6.8. Human shampoo is closer to 5.5, which strips the acid mantle and leaves the coat dry and itchy. We balance every wash to the skin it is made for.

The Meow range is formulated for cats — never the dog formulas. Cats over twelve weeks only; ask the Care Squad if your cat is on medication.
