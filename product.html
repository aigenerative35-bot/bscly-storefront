BSCLY Neem Herbal Shampoo for dogs and cats is enriched with neem, aloe vera and tea tree oil to gently cleanse the skin and coat, removing dirt, impurities and unwanted odours. Its herbal formula helps maintain coat freshness while leaving fur soft, clean and manageable.

  • Suitable for dogs and cats of all ages
  • Tear-free, pH-balanced, gentle enough for regular baths
  • No synthetic dyes, no camphor, no zinc oxide
  • 1 litre value size for multi-dog homes, groomers and shelters

1. Dilute 1:5 with water — one part shampoo to five parts warm water.
2. Wet the coat fully, then massage in from the neck back, avoiding eyes, ears and nose.
3. Leave for three minutes so the herbs can work, then rinse until the water runs clear.
4. Towel dry, brush through, and finish with a paw balm or a light mist.

Neem (Azadirachta indica) extract, aloe barbadensis leaf juice, tea tree (Melaleuca alternifolia) oil, coconut-derived cleansers, glycerin, panthenol, demineralised water, citric acid (pH adjuster), sodium benzoate & potassium sorbate (preservation).
